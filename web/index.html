Tips

  • A patch is nodes wired into the Output. Each node is a picture: a formula of x, y and t. It all compiles to one fragment shader and plays live, looping.
  • Wire by dragging from a node's right edge onto another node (or onto one of its inputs). The receiving node reads the wire as $name: $a is the input here, $a[x2,y2] somewhere else, $a[t2] at another time.
  • Add nodes from the bar above the desk. With a node selected, Warp, Mask, Displace, Blend and Time take it as their input.
  • Sequence paints its inputs in order, each while it is alive, blended onto what is below: debut and duration (in a node's Lifetime) say when; last follows the previous input.
  • Desk: drag to select, Delete removes, ⌘Z undoes. Pan with the wheel or by dragging with Space or Option held; zoom with a pinch or ⌘-wheel. On a touch screen drag the desk to pan, pinch to zoom, press and hold to select.
  • Click the picture to pause; the slider scrubs. Render movie… makes an MP4 or PNG frames; Save shader is a page that plays the loop by itself; GLSL pastes into Shadertoy.
  • Open… (or drop a file on the page) reads a .fragtime patch or imports a QTS .can script; dropped images are added for Image nodes. The patch stays in this browser between visits; Save downloads it. ⌘D duplicates the selection.
